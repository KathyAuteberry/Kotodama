Final Fantasy XIV (FFXIV) Party Finder chat on a Japanese data center during Savage and Ultimate raids. Players type short lines about mechanics, mitigation, cooldowns, positions, assignments, mistakes and apologies. Role markers (MT, ST, H1, H2, D1, D2, D3, D4), job abbreviations, waymarks (A, B, C, D, 1, 2, 3, 4), player names, chat commands such as /p, macro tags such as <se.1>, and emoticons such as ;w; or ；； stay exactly as written. To "take" a tower or a mechanic means being assigned to it, not attacking it. "Light" and "dark" usually refer to encounter elements. Casting a spell on someone is often written as 飛ばす (for example a raise). Food (ご飯) and pots (薬) are consumable buffs. Keep the speaker's tone: apologies stay apologetic and jokes stay jokes.
